The problem

Math and physics aren't chat problems.

Students learn these subjects by watching someone work through them — stroke by stroke, question by question. Today's tools can't do that.

Text answers skip the work

Chatbots hand over solutions as paragraphs. But understanding lives in the derivation — the part that never gets drawn.

Video can't answer back

Recorded lectures move at one speed and can't be interrupted. The moment a student gets lost, the lesson is over.

Great tutors don't scale

One-on-one teaching is the gold standard — and the most expensive, scarcest format in education.

The product

A teacher at the board, not a bot in a box.

CHALK pairs a realtime voice model with a deterministic drawing engine, so explanation and illustration happen as one act.

Speaks and sketches at once

Voice and ink stay in lockstep. Each stroke lands with the sentence that explains it — the way a teacher writes while talking, not before or after.

Interruptible by design

A student can jump in mid-stroke. The pen freezes exactly where it is, CHALK answers, then resumes the same stroke — never restarting, never skipping.

Checks for understanding

CHALK asks its own checkpoint questions — “Where does the curve peak?” — and waits for the student's answer before advancing the lesson.

Any topic, on demand

Name a topic — chain rule, circuits, projectile motion — and a validated lesson stream generates the board step by step. Nothing unvalidated ever reaches the student.

How it works

From question to whiteboard in seconds.

STEP 01

Name a topic

“Teach me projectile motion.” CHALK plans a bounded, multi-step lesson and validates every drawing instruction before a single stroke renders.

STEP 02

Watch it teach

The board draws itself while the voice explains — diagrams, axes, and equations appearing in sync with the narration, first ink in under six seconds.

STEP 03

Jump in anytime

Interrupt with a question mid-stroke. CHALK freezes the ink, answers out loud, checks understanding, and resumes right where it stopped.
